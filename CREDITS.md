# Credits

## Textures

All texture sets are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free for any use, no attribution required. They are credited here anyway. Each set lives in `assets/textures/<name>/` as 2K JPGs: colour (`diff`), OpenGL-style normal (`nor_gl`), and ambient occlusion, roughness and metal packed into one map (`arm`).

|---|---|---|

| [damaged_plaster](https://polyhaven.com/a/damaged_plaster) | Amal Kumar | Walls broken back to brick |
| [worn_cracked_plaster](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_cracked_plaster) | Amal Kumar | Ceiling damage |
| [checkered_pavement_tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/checkered_pavement_tiles) | Amal Kumar | Ward floor |
| [concrete_floor_worn_001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001) | Dimitrios Savva, Rico Cilliers | Concrete under lifted tiles, stair treads |
| [white_bricks](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_bricks) | Rob Tuytel | Tunnel vault and stair shaft (tinted toward the yellow Markham brick) |
| [worn_brick_floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_brick_floor) | Amal Kumar | Tunnel floor |
